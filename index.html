How the numbers are worked out
  • Source: BigQuery e-smart-433113.region_data.sales_all_retail, rows with ket_po = Non PO and Product_Category = Device. PO rows are pre-order bookings that are reversed when the phone is billed, so Non PO already holds the sale.
  • Same selling days: day 1 is each series' first sale. iPhone 18 covers 16 Oct – 9 Nov 2026 (25 days); iPhone 17 covers the same 25 days from 17 Oct 2025 (to 10 Nov 2025).
  • Only iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max and iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max are counted, because iPhone 18 is only sold in those two models. iPhone 17 base, 17E and Air are left out.
  • Filters: Model type detail picks Pro or Pro Max for both series. Business unit, LOB and TSH filter both series by each store's current mapping.
  • Units are net quantity (returns deducted). Value is total nett amount including tax. ASP = value ÷ units.
